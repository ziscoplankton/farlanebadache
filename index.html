Belief

Business is Human

Helping others has never felt like work. Throughout my career, I have always been drawn to understanding people, what motivates them and helping them overcome challenges to create moments that leave an impact.

From customer service to leadership, every experience has reinforced one of my belief: businesses are not just made of products, systems or numbers. They are shaped by the people behind them and the people they serve.

I have learned that the best improvements come from understanding before acting. Listening, observing and adapting have always been the foundation of how I approach challenges.

Always learning

Business will continue to evolve, but one thing will remain certain: people will always be at the centre of it. Products can change, systems can improve and technology will continue to advance, but without people there is no purpose behind any of it. Without stock there are no sales, and without people there are no jobs.

I built this website because I wanted a place to bring together the experiences, lessons and approaches I have developed throughout my career. Since 2011, I have worked across customer service, leadership, operations, technology and building my own business. Each experience has taught me something different about people, businesses and the challenges behind making things work. This website is a way to share those lessons and support people who are building something meaningful.

I help people tackle challenges, improve the way they work and find practical methods to move forward. Sometimes that means working in a business to understand the reality as no improvements can be made unless you are working from the inside and then apply my experience. Sometimes it means working on a business to fight assumptions, create structure and bring ideas to life. The common thread is always understanding the person behind the business first.

Experience matters when it has been tested and the experience shouldn’t be measured by years or titles alone. I have worked in many environments with customers, teams, operations leaders, product niches and businesses structures that forms knowledge and experience. Always on my toes and learning from mistakes to find solutions when there are no perfect answer.

Farlane Biscuiterie came to an end during COVID, a period that changed the way many small businesses operated and forced difficult decisions about the future. While the business itself stopped, the experience stayed with me. Building something from the ground up taught me what happens behind the scenes: making decisions with limited information, creating systems that work, supporting people and constantly finding solutions when things do not go as planned. It became one of the most valuable experiences of my career and continues to influence how I approach challenges today.

I am open to opportunities where my experience can contribute. I have never measured my work only by a position, title or financial outcome. What matters to me is creating value, learning through challenges and making a meaningful difference. Whether that is through a role, a project or supporting someone building something of their own, I am interested in opportunities where my experience can be useful.

No, I do not consider myself an expert in everything. I believe expertise comes from experience, curiosity and continuously challenging yourself. The more you learn, the more you understand how much there is still out there to grasp on. My strength comes from connecting different areas together and adapting my approach to different situations. When something requires knowledge outside my scope, I believe the right answer is finding the right people rather than pretending to have every answer.

If there is a challenge, idea or opportunity you want to explore, I will take the time to understand where you are, what you are trying to achieve and what may be preventing progress. If I believe I can contribute, I will. If I cannot contribute meaningfully, I will be honest about that too.
